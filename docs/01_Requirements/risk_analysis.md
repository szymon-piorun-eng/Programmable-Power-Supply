# Risk analysis

This document identifies, assesses, and proposes mitigation measures for potential risks associated with the project.

## 1. Methodology

- Probability in scale 1-5, where 1 is almost impossible to occur with proper use and 5 is almost certain to occur.
- Impact in scale 1-5, where 1 isn't damaging and 5 is permanently destroying the system.
- Risk level as multiplication of **probability** and **impact** where 25 is critical risk and value up to 5 is acceptable.
